🔬 Methodology

How this was researched ▾

Specifications, pricing, and performance figures were compiled from manufacturer spec sheets (Apple, ASUS, Lenovo, Dell) and independent lab reviews from NotebookCheck, Tom's Hardware, Tom's Guide, TechRadar, and XDA Developers, current as of mid-2026 configurations.

Each laptop was scored 1–10 on six criteria (Price value, Performance, Battery Life, Portability, Display, Build Quality) based on reported benchmarks, real-world battery test hours, chassis weight/thickness, panel technology, and build materials. Scores reflect a synthesis of multiple reviews rather than a single source, since real-world battery figures especially vary by test methodology and workload.

The overall verdict is calculated as a weighted sum: each criterion score is multiplied by the slider weight you set (0–10), then normalized to 100. Moving a slider to 0 removes that criterion from the verdict entirely, letting you simulate "I don't care about X" scenarios instantly.

This tool is for educational comparison purposes. Prices and rankings fluctuate with sales, currency, and regional availability — always check current listings before purchasing.
