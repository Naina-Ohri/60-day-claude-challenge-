📖 Onboarding Guide

  1. Explore Workflow Library for 10 ready-made AI workflows.
  2. Use Prompt Builder to assemble custom prompts from 9 blocks.
  3. Turn any prompt into an autonomous loop with the Loop Builder.
  4. Star workflows to pin them in Favorites; save custom ones in Saved Workflows.
  5. Track your challenge in the Dashboard day grid.
  6. Export your library as JSON to back it up or move devices.
