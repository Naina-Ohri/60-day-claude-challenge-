## Key Learnings
- Adaptive interview simulations create more realistic preparation than fixed questionnaires.
- Challenging every claim helps identify weak explanations and missing evidence.
- Confidence grows through continuous follow-up questions and structured storytelling.
- Personalized feedback is more effective than generic interview advice.
- Progress tracking and defense reports make it easier to focus on areas that need improvement.
