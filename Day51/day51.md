# Day 51 – Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## Overview
For Day 51 of the ABTalks 60-Day Claude Challenge, I began my 10-day capstone project by working with Claude to plan and define a realistic product that can be built from idea to deployment. The session focused on product discovery, project scoping, and creating the complete documentation required before development begins.

## Files Included
- `01-Executive-Summary.md`
- `02-PRD.md`
- `03-Implementation-Blueprint-Day2-10.md`
- `04-Pitch-Deck.md`

## Deliverables
- Executive Summary
- Product Requirements Document (PRD)
- Implementation Blueprint (Days 2–10)
- Project Pitch Deck

## Key Learnings
- Learned how to validate and scope a product idea before starting development.
- Understood the importance of creating a Product Requirements Document (PRD) as the project's foundation.
- Learned how implementation blueprints help organize development into manageable milestones.
- Explored how a pitch deck communicates a product's vision, users, problem, and solution.
- Gained insight into planning an end-to-end software development lifecycle before writing code.

## Project Selected
**PrepGenie** – An AI-powered career preparation platform that helps students optimize resumes, practice mock interviews, and prepare for placements through personalized AI guidance.
