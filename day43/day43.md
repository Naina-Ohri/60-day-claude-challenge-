## Key Learnings

- Learned how AI can be used to design complete end-to-end workflows instead of solving isolated tasks.
- Understood the importance of breaking complex processes into clear stages with defined objectives and outputs.
- Explored how different AI tools can be matched to specific workflow stages for maximum productivity.
- Gained experience designing interactive productivity applications with progress tracking, diagrams, and actionable insights.
- Observed how automation thinking helps identify repetitive tasks that can be streamlined using AI-powered systems.
